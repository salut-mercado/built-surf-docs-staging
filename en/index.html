Two applications, one business

Section titled “Two applications, one business”

Salut ERP management —🌐 Web · 🖥️ Desktop— is where you prepare products, assortments, prices, and stores; control inventory; and review sales and reports. Open it in a browser or from within Desktop.

Salut ERP Desktop is the Windows application that also provides checkout and connections to printers, the cash drawer, scales, customer displays, and payment terminals.

New to Salut ERP? Follow the quickstart. For a specific task, choose its section from the menu.
