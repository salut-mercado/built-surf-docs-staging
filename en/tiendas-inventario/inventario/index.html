Stores and inventory

Available in: 🌐 Web · 🖥️ Desktop

A store brings together its address, type, assortment, price list, terminals, and inventory. Before you record a revision, check that you are in the right store: the one you opened from Stores in the side menu. On a device set up as a POS, only that POS’s store appears.

Prepare a store

Section titled “Prepare a store”
  1. Open Stores and select a store to see its overview. To change its details, choose the pencil icon next to its name. To create a new store, choose Create Store in the side menu. That option depends on your account’s access, and it does not appear on a device set up as a POS.
  2. Under Address, complete every field of the location. When you create the store, all of them are required.
  3. Under Contact, choose the Store type: Supermarket, Fruit shop, or Butcher. Enter the Store name, the Store legal name, and the Tax ID. When you create a store, all three are required. When you edit it, the legal name is still required: without it, Update Store stays disabled. Existing stores stay supermarkets until you change their type.
  4. Under Operations, check the Working hours. A new store starts with hours of 9:00 to 18:00 every day. The Currency is under Contact: it is EUR, and you cannot change it for now.
  5. Under References, assign the Assortment and the Price list. Both are required to create the store.
  6. Choose Create Store or Update Store and check that the store’s POS shows the expected products.

Understand stock

Section titled “Understand stock”

A SKU’s stock in a store changes through these movements. It is not a number you edit directly.

  • Sale: reduces inventory when the sale syncs. Online, that happens when payment completes; offline, when Desktop syncs again.
  • Inflow: increases stock when you submit a supplier delivery.
  • Revision: sets stock to the counted quantity.

Receive a supplier delivery

Section titled “Receive a supplier delivery”

A supplier delivery records the goods that arrive at the store, together with the supplier’s invoice. Stock goes up as soon as you submit the delivery.

  1. Open Stock Movement and choose Create supplier delivery.
  2. In New Delivery, choose Start from Scratch. Salut ERP creates the draft at that moment, even if you have not added anything yet.
  3. Under Supplier, choose Select a supplier and find the supplier under My suppliers. Choose Select. If the supplier is not there, switch to Supplier library, find the supplier, and choose Use profile. Salut ERP adds it to My suppliers and selects it.
  4. Under Invoice Ref., enter the invoice reference, for example FAC-2026-01842.
  5. To keep the invoice with the delivery, choose Files. The Attached Documents panel opens. Choose Add Files and pick the file.
    • While a file is uploading, Submit Delivery does not send the delivery. Wait for the upload to finish.
    • If an upload fails, submitting shows Fix failed attachment uploads before submitting. Remove the file that shows the red alert icon with the X that appears when you point at its row, then add it again with Add Files. If it fails again, remove it: you can submit the delivery without the file and contact the Salut team.
  6. Choose Choose SKUs. By default, the list shows only the packs linked to the supplier, and it is empty if the supplier has none. If the pack that arrived is not listed, switch the filter to All SKUs. Each SKU appears on one row per pack (Pack column). Select the pack that arrived and choose Add selected.
  7. In each row, enter Qty and Price. Qty counts how many packs arrived, not single units. If a box of 12 arrives and you choose the box pack, enter 1, and stock goes up by 12. Salut ERP calculates the line Total.
  8. Check the VAT on each line and the VAT included switch (see below).
  9. Check the Inflow total below the lines, and compare each line total with the supplier’s invoice before you submit.
  10. Choose Submit Delivery. The delivery is received at once, and the quantities are added to the store’s stock.

VAT included shows whether the price you type already includes VAT. When you choose the supplier, the switch takes that supplier’s usual setting. If you change it, an overridden label appears. With VAT excluded, Salut ERP adds each line’s VAT to the delivery total, so the total depends on the option you choose.

If you change a line’s VAT and submit the delivery, the SKU’s VAT in the catalogue changes too. That VAT applies to future sales of the product.

Once you submit it, the delivery is no longer a draft and appears in the Inflow Qty card on Stock Movement. The Inventory page shows the updated stock for each product.

Save and resume a draft

Section titled “Save and resume a draft”

Choose Save Draft if you are not finishing the delivery now. Wait for uploads to finish before you choose it, as you do before submitting: a file still uploading is left out of the saved draft, and Salut ERP shows no message. Unsaved changes are lost when you leave the page or reload it. The saved draft appears under Supplier delivery on the Stock Movement page, with its invoice reference and supplier. Choose it to resume.

Each time you choose Start from Scratch, a draft is created in the list, even if you do not save or submit it. Discard only closes the editor: the draft stays in the list, and the app does not let you delete it. To avoid piling up drafts, resume an existing one instead of starting a new one.

Upload + Auto extract is available only to some accounts. It sends invoices or photos, up to 10 files of 20 MB each, so Salut ERP can prepare a draft in the background. Each store can have one pending upload at a time. Salut ERP notifies you when the draft is ready.

Start a revision

Section titled “Start a revision”

A revision counts the store’s actual stock. You work on a draft, and the inventory does not change until you finish it.

  1. Open the store, choose Inventory, and then choose Start revision. If a draft is already open, the button reads Resume revision.
  2. Scan the barcode of each SKU, with a scanner or the camera button, to add it to the draft. Salut ERP handles each code as follows:
    • If the SKU is already in your organisation’s catalogue, it is added to the draft. If it is outside the store’s assortment, it is also added, with an Outside assortment badge.
    • If the barcode is not in the catalogue, Salut ERP looks it up in the global library. If it finds it there, it adds the SKU to the catalogue and to the draft without further steps.
    • If neither has it, a dialog opens to create the item, with a name, VAT, and category. VAT is preselected at 21%. When you confirm, the item is created in your organisation’s catalogue and added to the draft. Choose Cancel to close the dialog without changes.
    • An item created in the dialog or taken from the global library is not yet in the store’s assortment and has no price. To get it ready, add it to the assortment and set a price, or, when you are online, scan it at a POS, which adds it to the assortment and asks for its price. See Assortments, prices, and labels.
    • If you would rather not scan, type the code or the name and choose the product from the list. Only SKUs active in the store’s assortment appear. For any other code, the field shows No matching SKU found; in that case, scan it to add it.
  3. In each row, enter the counted quantity under Counted. When you scan a barcode, Counted does not start at 1: it starts from the Current value and adds the units in the scanned pack. Each further scan adds again, so overwrite the value with the quantity you actually counted. The Diff column shows the change from Current. To remove a row, choose the trash icon.
  4. If a row shows a Conflict badge, it adds to the Blocked rows count in the summary, and Finish revision stays disabled until you remove it. The app does not let you fix the row: remove it with the trash icon, and contact the Salut team to review that product’s stock. If scanning a SKU shows Add failed with a message about a block, that SKU has several stock entries in the store, even if Inventory shows only one, and the revision cannot include it. Contact the Salut team too.
  5. Choose Finish revision. Salut ERP sets stock to the counted quantity and keeps the revision in the store’s history.

You can change a row’s VAT. If you do, Finish revision saves that VAT to the SKU in the catalogue. That VAT applies to future sales of the product, as it does for a supplier delivery.

To cancel a draft, choose Discard revision and confirm. The inventory does not change.

What Salut ERP does automatically

Salut ERP keeps every movement in the history. A revision sets stock to the counted quantity; it does not erase earlier history.

Review past revisions

Section titled “Review past revisions”

Choose the Last revision finished link on the Inventory page to open Revisions. That link appears only when the store has at least one finished revision. On Revisions, filter by All, Finished, or Cancelled, and choose a revision to see the final counted quantities.

Review movements for a period

Section titled “Review movements for a period”

The Stock Movement page summarises the store’s stock in and out for a period. Choose Today, This Week, This Month, or Custom. The cards show Inflow Qty and Outflow Qty, Sales Revenue, Realized COGS, Gross Profit, and P&L Trust. P&L Trust shows whether the sales and profit data is Trusted, in Warning, or Unavailable, and how many warnings there are. Below, the table shows each SKU with its Raw Movement, Realized Sales, COGS / Profit, and Open → Close inventory. If sales or profit data is unavailable for the period, or needs review, a notice says so.

If a quantity does not match, review each SKU’s row in the period’s table before making another revision. See Troubleshooting.
