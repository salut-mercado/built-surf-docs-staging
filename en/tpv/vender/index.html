Selling with the POS

Available in: 🖥️ Desktop · Windows

Open the POS

Section titled “Open the POS”

Before you sell, set up the register’s POS terminal under Settings → Hardware, on the POS tab (see the settings reference). Until you do, the POS button does not appear in the top bar and F12 does nothing. When the POS terminal is ready, Settings → Hardware → POS shows Ready for sales.

Sign in to Salut ERP Desktop and press POS in the top bar, or press F12. The store assigned to this register opens. Before you start, check the indicators at the bottom left of the screen. Confirm that the connection, the printer, and any other devices you use are active.

If the POS button is disabled, the store assigned to this register is not in the selected organisation. Switch organisation and try again.

If POS Terminal Not Bound appears when you open the POS, this computer is not set up as the POS terminal for that store. Choose I Understand. If this computer is the POS terminal of another store, press F12 to return to this register’s store. If this computer has no POS terminal set up, set one up as described above. Without a POS terminal, the register cannot complete any payment, and Salut ERP shows no message when you try to pay. See the settings reference.

Selling screen

Section titled “Selling screen”

The screen has three areas. Categories are on the left, and you can collapse them with the icon in the header. Items are in the middle. The search box accepts a name or a barcode. The cart is on the right, with the subtotal, tax, and total below it.

Above the list of categories is All Items. If the POS has favourites, Favorites appears before All Items, and the POS opens on that view, unless it restores the category you were last in. Weighted items and Uncategorized appear only when there are items of that kind. Each of these filters the grid. On a register with a POS terminal set up, the heart icon next to the search box opens favourites management for the register. While you manage them, that icon becomes the save icon (its tooltip says Save).

With Shopping mode on (F6), the register charges each item’s special price where one exists. The grid takes on a tint only to show that the mode is on. The tint does not change any price. Press F6 again to turn it off. When you press Pay, shopping mode locks for that cart until the sale completes, you empty the cart, or you resume a saved cart. Saving the cart with F8 also turns the mode off. Cancelling the payment does not unlock it. With When the cart appears set to While scanning (the default, under Settings → Hardware → Second screen), the button is disabled as soon as the cart holds an item with a special price, and F6 does nothing.

The bottom bar holds the action buttons, each with its key: Shopping mode (F6), Open Drawer (F9), Item (F7), Weight (P), and Recent sales (F10). Open Drawer is active only when a printer is chosen and connected, the switch on the Printer tab is on, and your account can open the drawer. The keys for the POS actions are the defaults; you can change them under Settings → Checkout → Shortcuts. Enter cannot be changed.

Build the cart

Section titled “Build the cart”
  1. Scan a barcode or find the product by name or code.
  2. Select the product card to add it. Repeat to add more units, or press + on the cart line.
  3. For an item that is not in the catalogue, press Item (F7). The name stays as VARIOUS. Choose the VAT rate (4%, 10%, or 21%, default 21%), enter the Price, and confirm with Enter.
  4. Review each line before payment.

If a scanned barcode is not in the organisation’s catalogue and you are online, the POS looks it up in the global library. If it finds it, it brings it into your catalogue and, if it has no price in the store, asks for one. If neither has it, Custom item opens with the notice Barcode not found in catalog. Set a price for the scanned code. Offline, an unknown barcode opens Custom item directly. Online, a catalogue item that is not in the store’s assortment is added to it, or switched back on if it was inactive, for every store that uses that assortment. If the item has no price in the store, Set item price opens (for a weighed item, its weight dialog with First price (EUR per kg) or First price (EUR per g)), and the price is saved to the store’s price list. If you cancel the price dialog, no price is saved and the store’s assortment does not change. An item already brought in from the global library stays in your catalogue.

To remove units, use the bin or minus button on the line. Removing a line changes only the open cart. Completed sales keep their original lines and details.

Correct prices and quantities

Section titled “Correct prices and quantities”

If this POS allows cart edits, each line shows a pencil next to its name. Tap the line to open the edit dialog. What the cashier can change depends on Price editing in the cart, which is Off by default. You find it under Settings → Checkout, in the Cart group. See the settings reference.

  • Off: prices come from the catalogue and cannot be changed in the cart.
  • Unit price: Edit price and quantity opens. Enter a new unit price and the quantity. The line total follows from the quantity.
  • Line total: Edit line total opens, with no quantity field. Enter what the customer pays for that line, and the unit price is recalculated from it.

These dialogs have no Save button: confirm with Enter, the numpad Enter key, or the return key on the on-screen keypad.

For weighed items, the dialog opens only when the setting is Unit price. It is called Edit price and weight, and it lets you correct both the unit price and the weight.

To remove a price adjustment, open the line and press Remove override. This button appears on lines with an adjustment, not on weighed items.

To empty the cart, press the bin icon in the header. If Confirm before clearing cart is on, Salut ERP asks you to confirm. Saved carts are not deleted.

Save and resume carts

Section titled “Save and resume carts”

Save a cart when you need to set a sale aside and serve another customer.

  1. With items in the cart, press the archive icon in the header or press F8. The cart moves to the saved list and the current cart is empty.
  2. To resume it, press the header button with the archive icon and the number of saved carts. This button appears only when the cart is empty. F8 also opens the list in that case.
  3. In Saved carts, each entry shows its date, time, total, and first items. Press Rename to give it a name, Delete to remove it (Salut ERP asks you to confirm), or Resume to continue the sale.

Saved carts stay on this computer and appear only for the store of this register.

Take payment

Section titled “Take payment”
  1. Press Pay. You can also use the decimal key on the numeric keypad.
  2. Choose Cash (key E) or Credit Card (key V). With the printer connected, choosing Cash opens the drawer.
  3. For cash, enter the amount received on the keypad or press a quick amount. If the customer pays the exact total, press Exact. Quick amounts and Exact only fill in the amount, so confirm with Enter. Salut ERP shows the Change due.
  4. For a card, the screen shows Tap, insert, or swipe and the amount. Press Complete (or Enter) only after the shop’s card reader has approved the payment. Salut ERP does not check the card reader at this step.
  5. Wait for Payment successful.
  6. To print the receipt for this sale, check the printer indicator at the bottom left, then tick Print Receipt. The box starts unticked. On Desktop it can be ticked even when the printer is disconnected, and if printing fails, the sale is still saved.
  7. Press Done.

Do not close Desktop before you press Done. The sale is saved at that point. If the application closes first, the cart stays open and the sale does not appear in the journal.

What Salut ERP does automatically

On completion, the sale, its lines, the payment, and the receipt stay linked. Inventory updates when the sale syncs: right away when online, or when Desktop syncs again after being offline.

Review the sales journal

Section titled “Review the sales journal”

In the POS, press Recent sales (F10) to review the sales journal saved on this register for its store. The first time, it opens with today’s sales. While the POS stays open, it returns to the last day you chose.

  1. Use Today, Yesterday, or the date picker to open a day.
  2. Check the number of sales and the day’s total. You can switch between list and grid views.
  3. For each sale, check the receipt number, synchronisation status, amount, sale time, payment method, and barcode.
  4. Press Reprint when you need another copy. You need Desktop and a connected printer. With the printer connected, Shift+F10 prints the receipt for the most recent sale saved on this register, without opening the journal.

The journal shows local sales kept on this computer, not the complete central history. To see sales from other registers, use Sales, reports, and analytics.

You can also create an invoice for a completed sale from this journal. Read Invoice a sale before you issue the first one.

Sell by weight

Section titled “Sell by weight”

Weighted products appear under Weighted items. Selecting one opens its weight dialog. If the store’s price list has no price for that item, the dialog first asks for the price labelled First price (EUR per kg), or First price (EUR per g) when the item is weighed in grams; see Assortments, prices, and labels. If a scale is connected, place one product on it and wait for a stable reading before you press Add line. If a scale is set up but not available, the panel shows Scale unavailable with the buttons Retry now and Enter weight manually. If no scale is active on this device, the dialog asks for the mass directly. Type it by hand in the unit the dialog shows, Exact mass (kg) or Exact mass (g) depending on the product, then press Add line. Each weight capture creates a separate line, even for the same item.

To weigh an item that is not in the catalogue, press Weight (P). The Custom weighted item dialog shows the fixed name VARIOUS. Choose the VAT rate (4%, 10%, or 21%, default 21%), then enter the price per kilogram and the mass in kilograms. With a scale connected, the mass comes from the scale: enter the price and, once the reading is stable, press Enter to add the line. Without a scale, press Enter after the price to move to the mass, and again to add the line.

Offline

Section titled “Offline”

Desktop can keep the operation ready for short interruptions. Do not close the application, and do not repeat a payment whose result is uncertain. When the connection returns, let Desktop synchronise and confirm the sale in Recent sales and on the store overview. Once it synchronises, its status changes to Synced.

Taking payment offline needs reserved receipt numbers. Under Settings → Hardware → POS, Offline sales should show Ready. If they run out, the payment does not complete, and Salut ERP shows no message. Connect and reserve more numbers from the same screen. If a message appears, see Troubleshooting.

If a sale does not sync, the top bar shows a counter with the number of sales in error. Open it to see POS sale sync failed, read the error for each sale, and fix the cause if you can. Then press the retry icon on that sale (its tooltip says Retry) or Retry all. A sale in error is not sent again on its own, so do not ring it up again.
