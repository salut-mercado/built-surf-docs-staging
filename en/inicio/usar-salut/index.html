Finding your way around Salut ERP

Use Salut ERP management in 🌐 Web or 🖥️ Desktop to prepare and review the business. The side menu groups its entries under Management (SKUs and Clients), My organisation (Overview, Assortment, and Prices), and Stores. Which entries you see depends on your account’s access. Your stores appear below Stores, identified by their address. On a device set up as a POS, only that POS’s store appears, and Create Store is hidden. Open a store and its quick actions lead to Point of Sale, Inventory, Stock Movement, Reports, and Print labels. Always check which store you are in before saving: the one you opened from Stores. SKUs, Assortment, and Prices cover the whole organisation.

On Windows, Desktop adds the counter tasks: the POS in the top bar, which appears once the POS is set up and which you can also open with F12, and Settings for devices. The gear icon in the side menu opens preferences for that computer and for Your account.

Salut ERP Desktop opens full screen. To use another application without closing Salut ERP, press the minimise icon, the last icon on the right of the top bar, after the bell. Desktop keeps running.

Changes saved in Web reach Desktop while the application is connected. If a recent change is missing, check the connection, choose the refresh button in the top bar (the circular-arrows icon) or Refresh in your account menu, and reopen the relevant screen.

Side menu controls

Section titled “Side menu controls”
  • Support opens Messages, usually with a screenshot of the page you were on attached to the draft. When there are unread replies, the button shows their number.
  • The button with your initials shows your session and lets you Switch account, Refresh, or Log out. If no accounts are saved on this computer, the option is Add account. In Desktop, the menu also has Quit Salut, which closes the application.
  • The gear icon opens Settings, with preferences for this device and Your account, which follows you to every device you sign in on.
  • The book icon opens this documentation.
  • The cloud-and-arrow icon checks for a new version.

The app version appears below the icons. If you belong to more than one organisation, an extra icon lets you switch organisation.

Notifications

Section titled “Notifications”

The bell in the top bar shows how many notifications you have not read. Open it to see the unread ones. Choose a row to open that notification in Messages, or choose Open messages to see them all.

In Messages, a notification counts as seen once it has been on screen for a moment. In the bell menu, each row also has a check mark that marks that notification as seen without opening it. Mark all as seen appears only when you have more than one unread notification.

Work safely

Section titled “Work safely”
  • Read the summary before confirming inventory movements or payments.
  • Do not close Desktop while it is finishing a payment or synchronising a sale.
  • Use simple names and unique codes so products are easy to find.
  • Choose Log out in your account menu (the button with your initials) when another person will use the computer.

Help inside the application

Section titled “Help inside the application”

Choose Support in the side menu to open Messages. Salut usually attaches a screenshot of the page you were on to the draft; you can remove it with the × on the screenshot or replace it with your own image using Attach image. Explain what you were trying to do, what happened, and which store you were in, then send it with the paper-plane button or Enter. Replies from the Salut team appear in the same conversation. For help during business hours, use WhatsApp from 09:00 to 18:00 Spanish mainland time.
