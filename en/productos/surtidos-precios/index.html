Assortments, prices, and labels

Available in: 🌐 Web · 🖥️ Desktop

An assortment contains the SKUs a store can sell. A price list defines what each costs. For a product to appear in the POS, it must be active in the store’s assortment and have a price. Weighed products are the exception: if they have no price, the POS asks for one when they are weighed.

Prepare the assortment

Section titled “Prepare the assortment”
  1. Open Assortment, then choose the three-dot icon on the right of the page header. Choose Create assortment and give it a name that shows what it is for.
  2. Under Assignment, choose Unassigned, then choose Add on each SKU the store will sell.
  3. Open the store under Stores, choose the pencil next to its name and, in References, choose the Assortment and the Price list. Save with Update Store.

Assigned SKUs show the Active switch. Turn it off so the POS stops loading the SKU in every store that uses this assortment. With a connection, turning it off is not enough to stop the SKU being sold: when you scan its barcode, the POS switches it back on in the assortment, or adds it if it was never assigned, for all those stores. If the SKU has no price in the store’s list, the POS asks for one and saves it to that list, which other stores may also use. The POS loads only active SKUs.

Set prices

Section titled “Set prices”
  1. Open Prices and choose a list in the selector, for example Turístico. To create another list, choose the three-dot icon on the right of the page header and choose Create price list.
  2. Find each SKU and enter its Base retail price.
  3. For the price the POS charges in shopping mode (F6), enter the Special price. If the row has no base retail price yet, enter it first. While shopping mode is on, the POS charges the special price of every item that has one. The tinted grid only shows that shopping mode is on. If you enter 0 under Special price, the POS uses the base price in shopping mode.
  4. Check the VAT % column for each row. Use Price status to find SKUs. No price shows those without a price yet, and At a loss shows variations whose SKU purchase price is higher than their base or special price. The purchase price belongs to the SKU, so all its variations share it. This filter uses saved prices, not changes you have not saved yet.
  5. Choose Save changes in the action bar. Until you do, changes exist only on screen: Discard, or switching to another list, removes them.
  6. Assign the list to the store in References, under Price list, and save.

Wholesale shows the purchase price from the preferred supplier. If the preferred suppliers do not give a single price, or there is none, it shows the lowest price. Under the price, a number shows how many of the stores that use this list have a purchase price. The purchase price belongs to the SKU, so all its variations show the same value. If a SKU’s purchase prices differ between stores, suppliers, or variations, the price is preceded by from and mixed replaces the number. Choose the arrow in the row to open Wholesale prices. There, each price groups the stores that have it, with their suppliers, and Preferred marks each store’s preferred supplier. Without a purchase price, the cell shows a dash.

To remove a variation’s price, open the actions menu on its row and choose Remove price. A base price of 0 is not saved. Removing a price takes effect straight away, without Save changes.

A weighed product is charged at the list price. If it has no price in the store’s list, the POS asks for the price in the weighing dialogue, under the label First price (EUR per kg) or First price (EUR per g), depending on the product’s sales unit. What you enter there is saved to the store’s price list as the base price, not only for that sale.

Print labels

Section titled “Print labels”

Label printing works only in Salut ERP Desktop. On the web, Prices does not show the print buttons, and the Print labels action on the store page opens the list with the items selected, but you cannot print from it. In Desktop, those buttons appear even when no printer is set up, so check the printer before you print.

Save your changes before printing. The label uses the price you see on screen, even if you have not saved it yet, but the POS charges only the saved price.

  1. Open Prices and choose the store’s list. If you open it from the store page with Print labels, every item in the store’s assortment is already selected.
  2. If anything is selected, choose Deselect all in the action bar. It appears only when something is selected.
  3. Tick one item with a price and choose Print labels in the action bar. Check the label’s size and alignment.
  4. Untick the test item, because printing does not clear the selection. Tick the items you want to print and choose Print labels again. Printing starts as soon as you choose the button. There is no separate send step.

Selected items without a price are not printed, and Salut ERP shows no warning. To print the label for a single row, use that row’s actions menu. It appears only on rows with a price.

What Salut ERP does automatically

The POS uses the assortment and price list of the store it is set up for. A saved change appears after synchronisation; a completed sale keeps the price that was charged.

If a product is missing from the POS, check in order: that the POS is set up for the right store, that the SKU is in the assortment and active there, that it has a price, and that you are connected. See Troubleshooting too.
