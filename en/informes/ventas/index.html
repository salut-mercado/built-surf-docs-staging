Sales, reports, and analytics

Available in: 🌐 Web · 🖥️ Desktop

Each store’s overview gives a quick view of the business. To create a workbook with exact figures, open the store, choose Reports, and open Sales report.

Analyse a period in the overview

Section titled “Analyse a period in the overview”

Open a store. Its period selector switches between Today, Yesterday, Week, Month, and Custom. For Custom, choose the start and end dates. Totals and charts use the store’s time zone. The selector changes the charts below it and the Z report. It does not change the sales cards for today, the week, and the month, or the Sales per Hour chart above it, which always shows today.

Above those cards, the comparison mode decides which earlier period they compare with:

  • Same time compares with the earlier period only up to the same moment. For example, on a Wednesday at 15:00, the Sales This Week card compares with last week up to Wednesday at 15:00.
  • Full period compares with the whole earlier period.

For the Sales Today card, the earlier period is the same weekday of the previous week. With Same time, the comparison runs up to the same time on that day.

Export a period

Section titled “Export a period”
  1. Open the store you want to analyse and open its sales report.
  2. Under From and To, choose the dates. The end date is included.
  3. If needed, filter by Suppliers, Categories, or SKUs.
  4. Under Sort by, choose the order. Turn on Include zero-sales rows to include SKUs with no sales in the period. Only SKUs with a stock record in this store are added.
  5. Review the Workbook summary and choose Export XLSX.

Filters change the workbook contents, not saved data.

Workbook contents

Section titled “Workbook contents”

The workbook has one row per SKU, with the SKU’s current name. Its columns include the barcode, the name, Stock end period, the quantity sold, the sales in euros, Avg sales / day, the SKU price (the average sale price for the period), and the Notes column. It has no unit, tax, line, or payment columns. If there are problems, the workbook adds a Warnings sheet.

Stock end period does not follow the report dates: it shows the store’s current stock, even when the period is in the past. Avg sales / day divides the units sold by the days the SKU had stock. If that cannot be worked out, the cell is left blank and the Notes column of that row explains why.

Check a sale

Section titled “Check a sale”

Do not use the workbook to confirm a sale. Open Recent sales on the POS, which lists the sales saved on that register. Check the receipt number, the total, and the sync status: Pending sync, Syncing, Synced, or Failed sync. Then open the store overview and check that the day’s totals have gone up.

Neither screen lists the products. Check them on the printed receipt, which only prints in Desktop: tick Print Receipt before you finish the payment or, with the printer connected, press Reprint in Recent sales to print another copy with its items.

If the sale is not on the overview, check the connection. Above the cards, a notice such as 2 not synced shows how many sales saved on this device have not been sent yet. Choose the refresh button next to Same time and Full period to retry the pending sales and the failed ones that can be sent again. If a sale stays on Failed sync, see Troubleshooting.

Z report

Section titled “Z report”

If you use the Z report from Salut ERP Desktop, its breakdown depends on the VAT breakdown per payment type option, under Receipts and labels. That page only appears in the desktop app. See the settings reference.

To print it, open the store in Salut ERP Desktop and choose Print report, next to the period selector on the overview. The report uses the selected period. The button only appears in Desktop.

Print it when closing operations according to your store’s process.

A useful check

After setting up a new product or device, make a test sale. In Recent sales, check the receipt number, the total, and the sync status. On the store overview, check that the day’s totals have gone up. Check the products on the printed receipt (in Desktop, tick Print Receipt when you take payment, or press Reprint in Recent sales).

If figures do not match, see Troubleshooting.
