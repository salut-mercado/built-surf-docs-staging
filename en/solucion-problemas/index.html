Troubleshooting

Start by reading the full message, checking which store you are in, and checking the connection. Do not repeat a payment or inventory action if you do not know whether it finished.

Sign-in and synchronisation

Section titled “Sign-in and synchronisation”
  • Cannot sign in: check connection, email, and password, then close and reopen the application. If Salut ERP asks for a 6-digit code, check your email and enter it.
  • PIN does not work: check you are entering the PIN of the account you chose. If it still does not work, choose Use another account, sign in with your password, then change the PIN under Settings → Your account, in Sign-in, with Change PIN. If it still fails, contact Salut (see Contact Salut).
  • You opened the form for another account by mistake: choose Back to saved accounts to select again without closing the application.
  • The password-reset link has expired, is invalid, or has already been used: reopen the sign-in screen (if you see saved accounts, choose Use another account first), choose Forgot password?, and request a new link.
  • The new password is rejected: enter at least 10 characters, follow the strength indicator, and avoid common words, names, patterns, or a password you used before.
  • A Web change is missing in Desktop: check that Web and Desktop use the same organisation, restore the connection, choose the refresh button in the top bar (the circular-arrows icon), and reopen the screen. Do not recreate the product while it synchronises.

Updates

Section titled “Updates”

The update buttons are in the side menu, above Support. The POS screen hides the side menu, and Settings shows only its own pages in it. To see the update buttons, leave the POS with the Salut logo at the left of the top bar, or choose Back in Settings.

  • “App update ready” appears with the Restart app button: the new version is already downloaded. Save any pending work, choose Restart app, and confirm with Restart now when you can close Salut ERP. If you are not ready, choose Cancel in the dialog.
  • “Interface update ready” appears with the Reload Salut button: save any pending work, then choose Reload Salut. The interface reloads straight away, without confirmation, in the same window, and the application stays open.
  • An update is downloading: you can keep working while it finishes.

Notifications

Section titled “Notifications”
  • The bell still shows a number: open the bell menu and choose the check mark on each row, or Mark all as seen if you have more than one unread notification. Reading a notification in Messages also marks it as seen.
  • You cannot find a notification: choose Open messages in the bell menu. Messages lists every notification by date.

Products and prices

Section titled “Products and prices”
  • Product missing from POS: check that the POS store has the product in its assortment with Active switched on and a price, and that the SKU was saved. Online, scanning a catalogue SKU that is not in that assortment, or that is inactive in it, adds it to the assortment or switches it back on, and asks for a price if it has none.
  • Code opens another product: Salut ERP does not save two SKUs with the same Produce Code, or two variations with the same barcode. Open the product that appears and compare its codes with the one you scanned.
  • Price is wrong: check the price list assigned to the store; completed sales keep the charged price.

Inventory

Section titled “Inventory”
  • Quantity does not match: review the sales and movements in Stock Movement and the last Inventory revision. If the SKU has several stock entries, do not correct it with a revision, because a revision rejects it. On the store’s Inventory page, press the arrow next to its quantity to see each entry’s quantity, and contact the Salut team. If it has one stock entry, correct it with a new revision that has the actual count, not by repeating an old operation. If the revision rejects it with Add failed, contact the Salut team too.
  • Recorded the same operation twice: do not make a third blind correction. Review the history in Stock Movement, then make a new Inventory revision with the actual count. It sets the stock to the counted quantity.

Sales and payments

Section titled “Sales and payments”
  • Unsure whether payment succeeded: if the customer paid by card, check the card reader first to see whether the payment was approved, then check the sale. Do not charge again until the status is confirmed.
  • Payment does not finish: check these in order:
    1. For a card payment, choose Complete once the card reader has approved the payment (or press Enter). For cash, enter an amount that covers the total and press Enter.
    2. Open Settings → Hardware → POS. It should show Ready for sales. If not, choose the Store and the Checkout, then choose Set up POS.
    3. If you are selling offline, the reserved receipt numbers may have run out. Then payment does not finish and no message appears: reconnect so the POS reserves more numbers.
  • “POS Terminal Not Bound” appears: this device has no POS set up for that store. Choose I Understand. If this device is the POS of another store, press F12 to return to this register’s store. If it has no POS set up, open Settings → Hardware → POS in Salut ERP Desktop, on the register’s computer, and set one up. You cannot set up a POS from Web. Until then you cannot take payment or print receipts.
  • A sale shows as failed: the receipt icon with a number in the top bar appears when there are failed sales. Open it and read the reason. If the reason is about authentication or access, sign in again, then press the retry icon on that sale (its tooltip says Retry), or choose Retry all. If Salut ERP rejected the sale’s data, retrying will not help: send the Salut team the receipt number. A network interruption does not make a sale fail: it stays pending and is retried automatically. Failed sales are not sent again automatically. Do not charge the customer again.
  • The POS button is disabled: the store assigned to this POS is not in the list of stores for the selected organisation. If you belong to more than one organisation, switch to the one that contains that store. If the button stays disabled, use Support in the side menu.
  • Desktop lost connection: keep it open, restore the network, and let it synchronise. Then confirm the sale in Recent sales: it should show Synced.
  • Total differs by cents: remember each line is rounded before the total is added.

Invoices

Section titled “Invoices”
  • Create invoice is missing: use Salut ERP Desktop, check that Settings → Hardware → POS shows Ready for sales, and open the POS while online so it reserves the invoice numbers for the current year.
  • Store invoice identity required: complete the store legal name, tax identifier, and address before issue.
  • No invoice numbers remain: open the POS while online so it reserves another block.
  • The invoice was issued, but its PDF failed: do not issue another. Open the invoice and use Retry PDF or Regenerate PDF (one of the two appears). If it stays on PDF pending, contact Salut.
  • There is a PDF conflict: if Invoice action failed shows the text “The invoice PDF path already contains different bytes”, the invoice is already issued: do not issue it again. Refresh Recent sales, open the invoice with Open invoice, move the file occupying the path to another folder, and press Retry PDF. With different text, refresh Recent sales and check whether the sale shows Open invoice before you try again.
  • An invoice issued offline may not have reached Salut ERP: do not issue it again. It is sent the next time you open the POS while online. If it still does not appear, note the invoice number and the store, and send them to Salut.

The Invoice a sale guide covers offline preparation, local archival, and replacements.

Printer and drawer

Section titled “Printer and drawer”

Reconnect cable and power, and check paper. The printer list updates itself, but it can take time: a few seconds when no printer appears, and up to a minute when one is already listed. If Test print, Cut, or Open cash drawer is disabled, check first that a printer is chosen and connected. Then check that the switch on the Printer tab is on. Open cash drawer also depends on your account’s access. If output is wrong, check the Zadig driver. The drawer normally depends on its printer connection. Test before another sale.

Scale and customer display

Section titled “Scale and customer display”
  • Scale port missing: reconnect the adapter, open Settings → Hardware, go to the Scale tab, press the circular-arrows icon next to Serial port (its tooltip says Refresh ports), and select the port again.
  • Unstable weight: clear the platform, wait for zero, and place one product on it. Manual entry is the fallback when the scale is unavailable.
  • Display not updating: on the Second screen tab under Settings → Hardware, confirm the correct display is selected, then choose Open second screen to reopen its window from Desktop.

Reports

Section titled “Reports”

Check dates, store, and filters. A new sale does not appear in the report until it finishes synchronising. The sales report only exports a workbook with one row per SKU, so to check a specific sale, use Recent sales in the POS.

Contact Salut

Section titled “Contact Salut”

If you are still blocked, include the store, task, exact message, and what you tried. WhatsApp +34 664 566 837 from 09:00 to 18:00 Spanish mainland time, or choose Support in the side menu to write to the Salut team in Messages. Replies appear in that same conversation.
