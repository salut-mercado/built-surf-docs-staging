Settings reference

Available in: 🌐 Web · 🖥️ Desktop

Settings has one page per topic and one shared list of pages on the left. Search settings filters settings by name. Back returns you to the page you opened Settings from.

Appearance, Checkout, Hardware, and Receipts and labels are saved on this computer, so repeat them on each POS. Your account details belong to your user and follow you to any device. Hardware and Receipts and labels only appear in the desktop app.

Appearance

Section titled “Appearance”
  • Color scheme: System, Light, or Dark. Each option shows a drawing of how it will look. These guides show the light theme.
  • Themes: one theme for light mode and one for dark mode, chosen from Salut, Easy on eyes, Olive, and Harbor. Themes only change colours.
  • Corner radius: a slider that makes corners across the app sharper or rounder.
  • Interface density: Normal or Touch. It is saved on each device. Until you choose one, the desktop app starts on Touch. A browser starts on Touch if the device has a touchscreen or the window is narrower than 768 px, and on Normal otherwise. These are only starting values. Once you save a choice, it is used instead.
  • Sidebar density: Comfortable shows full labels and Compact shows icons only. It appears on wide screens.
  • Blur behind dialogs: a slider from Off to 16 px.
  • App size: from 80% to 125%. Desktop app only.
  • Language: English or Español, under Language and time.
  • Show clock: shows or hides the time in the top bar, also under Language and time.

Checkout

Section titled “Checkout”

  • Confirm before clearing cart: asks before removing all items. If you turn it off, the cart clears straight away.
  • Price editing in the cart: Off, Unit price, or Line total. With Line total, the cashier types what the customer pays for that line. With Unit price, the cashier types a new price for the item.
  • When the quantity changes: appears only when you choose Line total. Keep unit price keeps the calculated price and lets the line total rise or fall with the quantity. Keep line total keeps the typed total.
  • After adding a custom item: Close the dialog or Keep it open. With Keep it open, the dialog clears after each item and waits for the next price, so the cashier can add several in a row.
  • Dialog position: Centered or Beside the cart. Decides where keypad dialogs open.
  • Sales in the top bar: Off, Number of sales, or Money taken. Shows a small chart of today’s sales next to the clock.
  • Shortcuts: keys for the most frequent POS actions. Focus a field and press the new shortcut. Salut ERP warns you if the shortcut is already assigned to another action or can’t be used. Each shortcut you change shows its own reset button. Reset all appears once any shortcut has changed, and returns every shortcut at once. Go home uses the Delete key and can’t be changed.

Hardware

Section titled “Hardware”

Hardware has tabs for POS, Printer, Second screen, and Scale. The other tabs stay locked until you set up the POS.

On the POS tab, choose a Store and a Checkout, then select Set up POS. The button turns on once a store and a checkout are chosen. If the store has only one active checkout, the POS selects it for you. When you are online, Set up POS also reserves the first 10,000 receipt numbers for offline sales. After that, the tab shows the store, the checkout, Offline sales, and Receipt number explained. The Checkout list and Set up POS depend on your account’s access.

If you choose a store and the checkout you need is not listed under Checkout, type its name under New checkout, then select Create and use. The POS creates the checkout and sets up this computer with it. The New checkout row appears according to your account’s access.

Offline sales shows Ready when the POS holds reserved numbers for sales without internet. If it shows Not set up, the POS holds no reserved numbers. While you are online, select Set up to reserve them. When it shows Ready, select Reserve next 10,000 to add another block. That button also needs a connection. When more than 40,000 numbers are left, the button shows 50,000 limit and reserves nothing more. If the numbers run out while you are offline, payment does not complete until you reconnect. When you are online, the POS reserves a new block once 500 or fewer numbers are left. If the numbers run out while you are online, it reserves one at payment.

Disconnect, in the Checkout row, leaves this computer without a POS and clears the receipt numbers reserved on it. It takes effect at once, with no confirmation. To sell again, set up the POS once more. The button depends on your account’s access.

On the Printer tab, choose the printer and the Receipt width. In the Test the printer row, select Test print or Test price label, and Cut cuts the paper. In the Cash drawer row, Open cash drawer opens the drawer. Follow the printer and cash drawer guide.

Check that the switch in the tab header is on. With it off, the chosen printer is ignored. The Test print, Test price label, Cut, and Open cash drawer buttons work only with a chosen, connected printer. They stay disabled with the switch off, if you have not chosen a printer, or if the chosen printer does not respond. Open cash drawer also depends on your account’s access. Reset to defaults appears once a printer is chosen or you change the receipt width. It clears the chosen printer and returns the receipt width and the Receipt font to their defaults. The other options under Receipts and labels do not change. If you changed only the font, the button does not appear.

If the tab shows No printers found, connect the printer to this computer. While the list is empty, it checks again every two seconds, so there is no need to reopen the tab. If it shows Printer controls unavailable, restart the desktop app. The print, drawer, and receipt width controls appear once the POS detects a printer.

On the Second screen tab, choose the customer screen, open it, and set how the cart and the thank-you message appear.

On the Scale tab, choose the model and the serial port, and check the connection state. Follow the scale and customer display guide.

Receipts and labels

Section titled “Receipts and labels”

This page only appears in the desktop app. It changes what this POS prints.

  • Receipt font: Open Sans, Space Mono, or Comic Neue for every receipt, label, and report this POS prints.
  • Price labels: Bold item name prints the item name in bold on price labels. Classic item name prints it in Arial, the way older labels did.
  • Z-report: VAT breakdown per payment type adds gross and VAT lines under each payment method.
  • Printed example: a sample receipt, price label, or Z-report that updates with every change and jumps to the printout that change affects. Use the Receipt, Price label, and Z-report tabs to switch between them.

Your account

Section titled “Your account”

Your account shows the email you sign in with, your first name, surname, phone, and birthday. When you change a detail, Unsaved changes appears. Select Save to confirm it.

Quick switch PIN lets you switch to this account with a six-digit PIN instead of your password. Open the setting to see how it works. If the account has no PIN yet, the panel shows Choose a PIN. Type the PIN twice and select Save PIN. With a PIN saved, select Change PIN to choose another, or Remove PIN to delete it. Keep the PIN somewhere safe and do not share it.

About Salut

Section titled “About Salut”
  • Application: the installed version.
  • Devices Connector: the version of the local bridge that printers and displays use. Desktop app only. On the web, the same row appears as Devices, with the value Web version.
  • Shell: whether you run the desktop app or the web app.
  • Updates: desktop app only. Check for updates looks for a new version of the desktop app.
  • Open source licenses: View licenses shows the notices for third-party packages.

To report a problem, use Support in the main sidebar.

Before changing a working POS

Note the current option and test a complete sale afterwards. Price editing, custom item flow, and devices directly affect checkout work.
